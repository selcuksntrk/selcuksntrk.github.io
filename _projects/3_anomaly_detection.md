An early warning system that detects anomalies in product metrics, helping companies identify issues quickly and minimize costs.

## Overview

Detecting problems early helps teams resolve issues before they escalate. This project monitors product metrics and alerts stakeholders when unusual patterns are detected.

## Key Features

- **Real-Time Monitoring** - Continuous analysis of product metrics
- **Early Warning System** - Proactive alerts before issues become critical
- **Cost Minimization** - Reduced losses by catching problems early

## Technical Approach

The system combines statistical methods with machine learning techniques to establish baseline patterns and identify deviations that may indicate potential issues.

## Impact

By implementing an early warning system, teams can reduce downtime and minimize costs associated with undetected issues.
